FOLIO 04 · BEFORE YOU START
Before you start

What you need on day one

Four things, and most shops already have three of them. We would rather you know now than find out halfway through setup.

The one that surprises people

A Facebook account

Not a page, not ads, not any posting. Just a plain Facebook account. WhatsApp Business accounts legally live inside a Meta business portfolio, and only a Facebook login can own one. An Instagram account on its own cannot connect WhatsApp, no matter the workaround you have been told about. If you run a professional Instagram, you very likely already have a linked Facebook account.

Your number

A business phone number

Your existing business number or a fresh SIM you own. It needs to receive one verification code during setup. If that number is currently signed in to the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app, it has to be removed from there first, because a number can live in one place only. The number stays in your name throughout.

Your facts

A catalog and your policies

Whatever you already keep. A Google Sheet, an Excel file, a PDF price list, or a Word document with your return policy and store hours. It does not need cleaning up first. If it is messy, we sort it out with you on the setup call.

Your people

The team who will reply

Names and emails for whoever handles customers, so we can invite them to the shared inbox. Three seats are included in the trial. They need no training beyond knowing how to use WhatsApp.

A word on timing

Connecting your number is same day. Separately, Meta verifies your business documents before your higher sending limits unlock, and that review sits with Meta, not with us. It is usually a few days and can occasionally run longer. You can use the full product throughout, so the trial is never blocked waiting on it. We tell you exactly where you stand at each step.

FOLIO 05 · QUESTIONS
Fair questions

Asked at every demo

Most askedDo I need a new SIM or number?

You can use your existing business number or a fresh SIM you own. Either way, the number stays in your name and your control. If you ever leave, the number and your data go with you.

Will my number get banned for broadcasts?

No grey market tricks here. Everything runs on the official WhatsApp Business API with Meta approved templates and built in opt out, which is exactly how WhatsApp wants bulk messaging done.

What do WhatsApp messages themselves cost?

Meta charges per message rates for template messages (marketing and utility), billed at Meta's India rates with no markup hidden inside. We show you the math before you send anything, and normal customer service replies within the 24 hour window are free.

My team is not technical. Can they use it?

If they can use WhatsApp, they can use YUSR AI. Setup is guided over WhatsApp itself, and most teams are replying from the shared inbox within an hour.

What happens after the 7 day trial?

We look at your week of real numbers together and quote a monthly plan shaped to your volume. If it is not worth it, you walk away, and your contacts export with you. No lock in, no drama.

Can the AI really handle Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. It detects the customer's language, replies in kind, remembers the preference, and even transcribes voice notes. A quick voice note just asking for the price gets a proper, typed out answer.

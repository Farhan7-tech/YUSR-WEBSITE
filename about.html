FOLIO 04 · CREDENTIALS
What Meta approval means

Why your number does not get banned

In July 2026, YUSR AI passed Meta's app review and was approved as a Tech Provider on the WhatsApp Business Platform. That is not a marketing badge you can buy. It means Meta reviewed the software and granted it live access to the WhatsApp Business API.

In practice, that is the difference between two kinds of tools. Unofficial tools automate a normal WhatsApp account and get numbers banned in waves. YUSR AI sends through the official API, with Meta approved templates and a real opt out, which is how WhatsApp intends bulk business messaging to work.

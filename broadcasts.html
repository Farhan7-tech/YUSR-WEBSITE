How it works

FOLIO 03 · RELIABILITY
Built to survive real life

One bad number doesn't kill 5,000 good ones

Fault tolerant

Bad numbers don't stop the batch

A wrong number or a Meta error on one recipient is logged and skipped. The other 4,999 messages keep going without interruption.

Crash recovery

Stuck sends are caught automatically

If a broadcast ever gets stuck mid send, it's automatically detected and marked failed instead of sitting there forever with no progress.

Retry & duplicate

Fix it, don't redo it

Retry sends again only to the recipients that failed. Duplicate prefills a new broadcast with the same settings for your next campaign.

FOLIO 04 · SAFE SENDING
Built to keep your number safe

Bulk, without the ban

Official API

No grey market tools

Everything runs on the official WhatsApp Business API. No cracked mods, no number rotation tricks, no risk of waking up banned.

Templates

Meta approved messages

WhatsApp only allows free form replies within 24 hours of a customer messaging you first. Anything beyond that legally requires an approved template. We handle the approval with you.

Opt out

STOP means stop, instantly

Reply "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE" to any conversation and that contact is auto flagged. Every future send checks this first and skips them, logged, not silently dropped.

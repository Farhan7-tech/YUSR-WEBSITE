FOLIO 03 · HOW TRACKING WORKS
The printed code never has to change

Why the QR isn't just a wa.me link

The image you print does not encode your WhatsApp number and message directly. It encodes a short tracked link on our own domain, which redirects to WhatsApp in a fraction of a second. That single design choice makes three things possible:

Real scan counts

Bots and link previewers are filtered out

Facebook, WhatsApp's own preview fetcher, and search crawlers all touch the link when it's shared. Only real human scans count toward your numbers.

Edit after printing

Change the message, keep the same code

Update the prefilled text any time. Every future scan of the already printed code picks up the new message instantly. No reprinting.

Safe to delete

A deleted code fails gracefully

If you ever remove a code, old printed copies simply redirect to the YUSR homepage instead of showing a broken link on someone's phone.

FOLIO 06 · REVIEW QR
Part 2 · Review QR codes

A different QR, for growing Google reviews

This is a completely separate system from the quick entry codes above, built to drive real Google reviews from happy customers while catching unhappy ones privately instead of letting them go public. Low ratings are never discarded, just routed to you instead of to Google.

Customer scans, rates first

The QR opens your public review page. Before anything else, the customer picks a star rating, 1 to 5.

4 or 5 stars → sent to Google

Routed straight to leaving a real review on your Google listing, with an optional starter template so they're not staring at a blank box.

1 to 3 stars → private feedback, and an alert to you

Instead of a public review, they see a private feedback form right there. You get a notification so you can follow up with that customer directly. Nothing goes public, nothing gets lost.

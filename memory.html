FOLIO 03 · VERIFIED
Never a fake "Ready" badge

Verified, not just uploaded

A file finishing its upload doesn't automatically mean the AI actually learned anything from it. A scanned PDF with no real text, for instance, produces zero usable knowledge. So we check.

Counted

Chunks are counted, not assumed

After processing, we count the real searchable pieces that landed in your knowledge base. Only when at least one is confirmed does the document get marked "Ready".

Honest failures

Empty documents are marked failed

A scanned image with no extractable text, a corrupted file, a timeout. Any of these gets an honest "failed" status and an amber warning, never a silent fake success.

Self healing

Drift gets caught automatically

Every time you open your knowledge base, it quietly rechecks that every "Ready" document still has real content behind it, and cleans up anything that doesn't.

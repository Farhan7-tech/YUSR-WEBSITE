FOLIO 02 · THE BASICS
How the inbox works

Run WhatsApp like a counter with a queue

Filters

All, Unread, Assistant, Takeover

Four tabs tell you at a glance what the bot is handling, what a human took over, and what's still unread. No hunting through one long list.

Ticks

Accurate delivery ticks

Grey single = sent, grey double = delivered, orange double = read. Kept accurate for messages sent by the AI and by your team alike, not just the ones you type yourself.

Tags

Stamp intent on contacts

Mark chats as hot lead, cod, wholesale, or anything you invent. Filter the whole inbox by tag in a second.

Canned replies

Type "/" for a saved reply

Your most common answers, one keystroke away. No retyping the same delivery policy for the fortieth time this week.

Templates

Reach a closed 24 hour window

WhatsApp only allows free form replies within 24 hours of a customer's last message. Outside that window, send an approved template instead, still from the same inbox.

History

Nothing gets lost

Full conversation history stays with the contact, whichever agent picks up next. Media is saved permanently, not just on Meta's temporary link that expires.

FOLIO 03 · TAKEOVER
AI to human handoff

Any teammate can step in, any time

Your AI assistant answers every conversation by default: pricing, stock, delivery, policy, all in seconds. Whenever you or a teammate wants to jump in personally, one tap takes over the chat: the bot goes silent, the full history is right there, and the customer never has to repeat themselves. If nobody explicitly releases it back, the conversation quietly returns to the bot after 4 hours of inactivity.

The AI handles the routine

Price, stock, delivery time, return policy. Answered instantly from your AI Memory, in the customer's language.

You take over with one tap

No trigger words to configure. Any agent can take any conversation over the moment they want to, and the bot stops replying immediately.

It hands back automatically

Release it manually when you're done, or let it auto return to the bot after 4 hours with no reply. Nothing sits in limbo forever.

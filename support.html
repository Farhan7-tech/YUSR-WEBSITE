FOLIO 04 · COMMON QUESTIONS

The questions asked most often

Do I need a new SIM or number?

No. Use the number you already give customers, or a fresh SIM you own. Either way the number stays in your name and your control. If you ever leave, the number and your data go with you.

Will my number get banned for sending broadcasts?

No grey market tricks are involved anywhere in this product. Everything runs on the official WhatsApp Business API with Meta approved templates and built in opt out handling, which is exactly how WhatsApp itself wants bulk messaging done. YUSR AI passed Meta's app review as a Tech Provider on the WhatsApp Business Platform in July 2026.

What do the WhatsApp messages themselves cost?

Meta charges per message rates for template messages (marketing and utility), billed at Meta's India rates with no markup hidden inside. We show you the math before you send anything.

From 1 October 2026 Meta also charges for service messages, which are your replies inside the 24 hour customer service window. Every business number gets 1,000 of those free each month, and Meta charges only from the 1,001st. Full plan pricing is on the pricing page.

My team is not technical. Can they actually use it?

If they can use WhatsApp, they can use YUSR AI. Setup is guided over WhatsApp itself, and most teams are replying from the shared inbox within an hour of getting access.

Can the AI really handle Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. It detects the customer's language, replies in kind, remembers that preference for next time, and transcribes voice notes. A quick voice note just asking for the price gets a proper, typed out answer back.

Why should I trust the AI's answers?

Every answer shows its source, the exact document and page or row it came from, so any reply can be audited back to where it came from. If a question falls outside what you have uploaded, the assistant says so honestly and hands the conversation to a human with full context attached, instead of guessing at a price or a policy.

I run more than one brand, or I am an agency with several clients.

Each business runs in a fully isolated workspace: its own WhatsApp number, its own data, its own agents, switchable from a single login. Data never crosses workspaces, and that isolation is enforced at both the application layer and the database layer.

What happens after the 7 day trial?

You pick one of the three plans, Starter, Growth or Scale, and the price is the price. Nothing is charged during the trial and nothing renews on its own until you subscribe. If it is not worth it, you walk away and your contacts export with you.
